What is Language

Alchemy? A transformational group course to observe and then change the way we use language, shifting the way we interact with life

Who is it for?

  • Anyone curious about their self-talk
  • Those who want to uplevel in their life
  • Entrepreneurs wanting to level up their business
  • The self-sabotager who wants off that ride
  • Mamas who want to pass on good habits to their children
  • Trauma or PTSD stuck folks who are ready to heal
  • People who want to learn more about the water body, frequency, neural networks, and our fascia
  • If you want to be able to speak your truth better and not feel shut down
  • Improving your relationship with your loved ones
  • Wanting to deepen your relationship with yourself
  • Curious about diving into your subconscious

What to expect

A one-month group container
We will move through one stage of alchemizing our language each week:

  1. Observation
  2. Cutting Ties
  3. Building the Bridge
  4. Completion & Integration
  • We will have 4 weekly informational & group coaching Zoom calls.
  • You will get an email each week with tools, practices, and logistics.
  • There will be a Telegram group for support, sharing, connecting, and answering questions in between sessions.

Why I Created Language Alchemy

When this container first nudged at me, it was born of a passion for playing with words, a love affair that has lasted my whole life. From the time I could write, I was hooked on creating worlds with poetry and storytelling. I knew the power of words and how you could elicit emotions with the right composition. I could create a mini world with the language, cadence, and delivery.

I will never forget when I first consciously realized I was using words against myself. I had been through a number of traumas and shocks to the system that made me numb in my body, and in a way to life. I became unconscious of the words I used to talk about myself, my life, and the world. After doing some healing work, I started the journey of occupying my body and life more. Instead of just going through the motions.

As I became more aware, I was shocked. The more I paid attention the more I saw how I was reinforcing a frequency over my body and life that was so negative. I recognized the words that were limiting my potential and in a way keeping out the things and experiences I actually wanted in my life. By seemingly subtle shifts, I could feel the energy opening up. It started inside of me. I was less tense. Real magic unfolded as I started to see it reflected in the world outside of me.

I have been tending to this skill set over the last 15 years. Experimenting with supportive tools. EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique), meditation, affirmations, mirror work, somatic movement, ritual, etc. all served my process and helped to anchor in the shifts I was wanting to make permanent features in my life.

I have been integrating these findings into my containers with my 1:1 clients over the years. Utilizing what fit where, adding in what I found really worked for me. While it has enriched their lives and my offerings, I was feeling a deep calling to go ALL IN. I wanted to create a container where we solely focus on language alchemy, and move through the rewiring process together. This topic has such depth and nuance. So, I wanted to create something that could honor that while giving the full download of all the gold I have gathered. Thus, Language Alchemy was born.
